# Online Giving Page

Give your congregation one simple page with every way they can give their tithes and
offerings — bank transfer details, PIX, alias, mobile wallets or a payment link — with a
**Copy** button next to each detail so nobody has to type an account number by hand.

HolyCRM doesn't handle the money: people give from their own bank or wallet app, straight
into your church's account. There are no extra fees from HolyCRM.

## Turning it on

1. Open **Giving Methods** (under Finance) and click **Add giving method**.
2. Pick the **Type** — the form then shows only the fields that type needs:
   - **PIX (Brazil)** — your PIX key, the account holder and the city. The page shows a PIX
     **QR code** and a **copia e cola** button, ready for any Brazilian bank app.
   - **Bank transfer — alias / CBU / CVU (Argentina)** — alias, CBU or CVU, holder, CUIT and bank.
   - **SPEI — CLABE (Mexico)** and **Bank transfer — CCI (Peru)**.
   - **Mobile wallet or instant-payment key** — Yape, Plin, Nequi, Daviplata, Bre-B, Pago
     Móvil, MB WAY, Bizum… with the number or key.
   - **Bank account (other countries)** — Chile, Colombia, Uruguay and anywhere else: bank,
     account type, account number, holder and tax ID.
   - **UK bank account — sort code** and **IBAN — SEPA (Europe)**.
   - **Online payment link** — Mercado Pago, PayPal, Stripe or any payment page.
   - **Other / instructions** — free text, for anything else.
3. Optionally add a **Title** (for example *Tithes and offerings* or *Missions fund*),
   **Instructions** (*"add the word OFRENDA as the reference"*), the **Currency** and an
   **Order**.
4. In [Church Settings](#/church-settings), tick **Public giving page** and save.

You can add as many methods as you want — for example one for tithes and another for a
building fund. **Hidden** removes a method from the page without deleting it.

## Sharing it

Once it's on, a **🎁 Give** button appears on your [Church Links](#/church-links) page and
your [Church Website](#/church-website). The **Giving Methods** screen shows the page's link
and a QR code you can download: show it on screen at offering time, print it in the bulletin
or post it in your WhatsApp groups.

Gifts made this way still need to be recorded in [Giving & Finance](#/finance) by your
treasurer, as with cash or any other transfer.
