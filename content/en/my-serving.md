# My serving

**My serving** (under your account) lists the dates you're serving in the next 8 weeks — the
service or activity, your role and the time.

For each date, tap **✅ I'll be there** or **❌ I can't make it** (you can add a short reason).
Your coordinator sees your answer straight away on the Rotas board, and if you can't make it
they get an email so they can find someone else. Changed your mind? Tap **Change**.

If you get an email or WhatsApp message asking you to confirm, it's the same answer — you can
reply from either place.

If the page says no member record is linked to your account, ask your church admin to link
your login to your member profile.

Want to be suggested for the times that suit you? Fill in [My availability](#/my-availability).
