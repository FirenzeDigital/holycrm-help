# Plans & Billing

Every new church starts with a **30-day trial** — the entire app unlocked, every module, no
limits on people, team members or locations. No card required.

## What happens after the trial

If your church hasn't moved to the paid plan by the time the trial ends, nothing is deleted
and nobody is locked out. Your church automatically continues on the **Free plan**:

| Free plan | |
|---|---|
| People | Up to 50 |
| Team members (logins) | Up to 2 |
| Locations | 1 |
| Included | Members, Visitors, Groups, Attendance, Events & calendar |

Everything else — Rotas, Finance, Bulk Email, Kids Check-in, event RSVPs, Reports, your
church website, Custom Fields, Church Links, and Custom User Access — becomes available
again as soon as your church moves to the paid plan.

## If your church is already over a Free plan limit

Say your church already has 80 people, or 3 team members, by the time the trial ends.
Nothing gets deleted. Records above the limit become **read-only** — visible, but you can't
edit them or add new ones — until either you're back under the limit or your church moves
to the paid plan.

**Deleting is always allowed**, even above the limit — it's the quickest way back to full
access yourself, without waiting on anyone.

A banner at the top of the app tells you exactly which limit(s) your church is over, and by
how much, for as long as it applies.

## Upgrading

Ready to move to the paid plan, or not sure which side of a limit your church is on? Reach
out to **support@holycrm.app** — a real person will help, usually within a day or two.
