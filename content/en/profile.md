# My Profile

Your own account settings — visible and editable by anyone with a login, regardless of
role or which church(es) you belong to.

- **Name and avatar** — how you appear throughout the app. If a "My church info" card
  (below) is showing, your name is edited there instead — see why below.
- **Change password** — enter your current password and a new one.
- **Change email** — this uses a verified flow for security: a confirmation link is sent to
  the **new** address, and the change only takes effect once you click it and confirm with
  your password. This protects you from someone silently redirecting your login email if
  your session were ever compromised.

Profile doesn't control your role or which churches you can access — that's managed by an
Admin or Manager at each church; see [Users, Roles & Permissions](#/users-roles).

If you belong to more than one church, use the church switcher elsewhere in the app to move
between them — your Profile itself is shared across all of them, since it's tied to your
one login, not to any single church.

## My church info

If your login is linked to a record in the congregation (common for the **Member** role,
but not exclusive to it), a second card appears with your contact details as a member:
phone, address, birth date, sex, and your name. Editing your name here — rather than
above — keeps it in sync with how staff see you everywhere else in the app, from
directories to giving statements.

This card simply doesn't appear if your login isn't linked to a member record — that's
normal for most staff logins, not an error. See [My Giving](#/my-giving) for your own
giving history, shown the same way.
