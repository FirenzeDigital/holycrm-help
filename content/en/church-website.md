# Church Website

A full public website for your church — beyond a single link-in-bio page: an About
section, service times, upcoming events, ministries, a Visit Us section with directions,
your latest message, and a "connect with us" form, generated automatically from data you
already keep in HolyCRM.

## Turning it on

1. Set a **Public Links Page** address from **Admin Settings → Church Settings** if you
   haven't already — this is the same address your [Church Links](#/church-links) page
   uses.
2. Go to **Admin Settings → Church Website** and choose a look under **Website**:
   **Modern**, **Classic**, **Minimal** or **Premium**. Picking one turns the site on at
   `/site.html?c=your-public-links-slug`; leaving it blank keeps the site off.

## Choosing a look

- **Modern** — a bold, colorful landing page with rounded pill buttons and soft gradients.
- **Classic** — a warm, serif-typeset layout that reads as traditional rather than
  "startup."
- **Minimal** — plain type and generous whitespace, no cover photo, letting your message
  carry the page.
- **Premium** — a more design-forward look with frosted-glass panels and subtle scroll
  animation.

All four show the same information — pick whichever fits your church's personality, and
switch any time without losing content.

## What shows on the page

- **About** — the paragraph from **Website About Text**, plus your **Website Cover Photo**
  and logo.
- **Next service and upcoming events** — worked out automatically from your
  [Events](#/events) and recurring [Ministry Activities](#/ministries), the same as on
  Church Links.
- **Ministries** — active ministries with their age range, so visitors can see what's
  available for their family before they even show up.
- **Visit us** — your **Website Address**, **Website Phone** and **Website Email**, with a
  Get Directions link. If your church has more than one [Location](#/church-settings) with
  its own address filled in, every location shows as its own card instead of a single
  block.
- **Latest Message** — paste a YouTube or Vimeo link into **Website Latest Message Video**
  to embed your most recent message right on the page. Any other kind of link still shows
  as a plain "Watch" button instead of a broken embed.
- **Connect with us** — the same short form as Church Links; whoever fills it in becomes a
  new [Visitor](#/visitors) in your follow-up list.
- Your social links, reused from [Church Links](#/church-links).

## Seeing how it's doing

The **Your website** banner on the Church Website screen shows the live URL, a view
count, and a QR code for print materials — separate from Church Links' own banner, since
they're two different pages people can visit.

## Using your own domain

Instead of a holycrm.app address, you can point your church's own domain (like
www.yourchurch.org) at your website — or your Church Links page, if you haven't turned
on a website yet.

1. On the Church Website screen, enter your domain under **Custom Domain** — usually
   with a "www." in front. (A bare domain with no "www," like yourchurch.org, needs DNS
   support most domain providers don't offer for free.) Save.
2. Click **Connect domain**. You'll see a DNS record to add — pass it along to whoever
   manages your domain (your registrar, or your church's IT volunteer) and have them
   add it exactly as shown.
3. Click **Check status** until it shows **✓ Connected** — this usually takes a few
   minutes once the DNS record is live, but can take a few hours depending on your
   domain provider.

Once connected, visiting your own domain shows your website (or Church Links page)
directly — no holycrm.app address needed.
