# Getting Started

Welcome to **HolyCRM** — the admin app your church uses to manage members, small groups,
ministries, rotas, events, attendance, giving and communication, all in one place.

This Help Center is meant to answer two kinds of questions: *"how do I do X"* when you're
just getting started, and *"why does it work this way"* when something doesn't behave the
way you expected. Use the search box above, or browse by topic in the sidebar.

## Logging in

1. Go to your church's HolyCRM login page and enter your **email and password**.
2. If you belong to more than one church, you'll be asked to pick which one to manage —
   you can switch churches later from inside the app without logging out again.
3. Forgot your password? Use **Forgot your password?** on the login screen. You'll get a
   reset link by email regardless of whether the address is registered, so no one can use
   that screen to check who has an account.

If you don't have a login yet, ask an admin or manager at your church to invite you — see
[Users, Roles & Permissions](#/users-roles).

## Finding your way around

The left-hand menu is grouped by topic:

- **Overview** — the Dashboard (a snapshot of your church's activity) and the Calendar.
- **People** — Members, Import Data, Small Groups, Visitors.
- **Ministries & Rotas** — Ministries, Ministry Members, and volunteer scheduling ("rotas").
- **Events** — one-time events and who's registered for them.
- **Attendance** — general attendance, Check-in, and Kids Check-in.
- **Communications** — Bulk Email and reusable Email Templates.
- **Finance** — Giving, transactions, categories and the Finance Dashboard.
- **Admin Settings** — Church Settings, Locations, Users, Tags.
- **Account** — your own Profile.

**You may not see every item.** What's visible depends on your role (Admin, Manager,
Volunteer or Member) and on any custom permission your church has set for you — see
[Users, Roles & Permissions](#/users-roles) if something you expect to see is missing.

## The four roles, in a nutshell

| Role | Typical use |
|---|---|
| **Admin** | Full access to everything, including Church Settings and inviting other users. |
| **Manager** | Day-to-day operations — members, groups, events, rotas, communications, finance — without the account-level settings an Admin has. |
| **Volunteer** | Narrow, task-focused access (e.g. taking attendance, running check-in) depending on what your church has granted. |
| **Member** | Read-only self-service — mainly your own Profile. |

A church can fine-tune this further per person; your actual access can differ slightly from
the table above.

## A typical first week

1. **Church Settings** — set your church's name, timezone, default currency and (optionally)
   a logo. See [Church Settings & Locations](#/church-settings).
2. **Bring in your people** — either add [Members](#/members) one at a time, or use
   [Import Data](#/data-import) to bring in a spreadsheet you already have.
3. **Set up Locations** if your church meets in more than one place — needed for accurate
   timezones and per-location settings.
4. **Create your Ministries and Small Groups** and add members to them.
5. **Add upcoming Events** and, if you run rotas (Sunday teams, tech, hospitality…), set up
   **Service Roles** and start filling **Rotas**.
6. **Try Check-in or Kids Check-in** before your next service so your team isn't learning it
   live.
7. **Send your first Bulk Email** — a welcome note or an announcement — once you have a few
   members with email addresses.
