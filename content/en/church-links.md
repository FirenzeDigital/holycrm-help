# Church Links

A single branded page — a "link in bio" — for your church's Instagram, TikTok or Facebook
profile: one URL with every link people actually need (service times, giving, socials,
your address) instead of one link that only goes to your website.

## Turning it on

Set a **Public Links Page** address from **Admin Settings → Church Settings** (letters,
numbers and dashes only, must be unique) — this turns the page on at
`/links.html?c=your-church-name`. The page's logo, brand color and address all live on
that Church Settings record; what actually shows on the page is managed here, in
**Church Links**.

## Managing links

**+ Add link** for each item you want on the page: a title, a URL, and an optional icon
(pick one from the picker, or type any emoji). Two extra settings worth knowing:

- **Order** — lower numbers show first.
- **Featured** — shows this one link as a larger card above the regular list. Meant for
  one timely thing at a time — "This Sunday's service," a holiday event, a fundraiser —
  not a permanent state for every link.
- **Hidden** — takes a link off the public page without deleting it, so you can turn it
  back on later exactly as it was.

## What visitors see

Besides your links, the public page also shows:

- **Next service** — worked out automatically from your upcoming [Events](#/events) and
  recurring [Ministry Activities](#/ministries), so you don't have to keep a "next Sunday"
  link up to date by hand.
- **"New here?"** — a short form; whoever fills it in becomes a new [Visitor](#/visitors)
  in your church's follow-up list, tagged with an online source, instead of just being a
  click you'll never follow up on.
- A **Share** button and a QR code for print materials — a bulletin, a welcome card, a
  sign in the lobby.

## Seeing how it's doing

The banner at the top of this screen shows your page's URL, a view count, and a QR code
(with a bigger version to download for printing). Each link's own **Clicks** column in the
table below tells you which links people actually use.
